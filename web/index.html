4 Results

Accuracy is how often a network's top guess was exactly the chord that came next in songs held back from training; similarity (sim.) gives partial credit for near misses, using Word2Vec chord embeddings (0 to 1). Describing chords by their notes helped less than expected: plain one-hot encoding came out best with every network, and the two proposed representations landed in between.
